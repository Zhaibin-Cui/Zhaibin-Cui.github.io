Interactive particle studies

The particle compositions are original, code-generated illustrations. The Transformer study follows token projections, causal attention heads, output projection, residual paths and feed-forward computation. The hand study uses an original articulated rig informed by published hand-rigging and dexterous-hand kinematics. These are conceptual visualizations, not measured model activations or robot-control results.

  • Vaswani et al. · Attention Is All You Need ↗
  • Shazeer et al. · Sparsely-Gated Mixture-of-Experts ↗
  • Jiang et al. · Mixtral of Experts ↗
  • The MoE study shows three parallel causal-attention heads inside one conceptual decoder block and 16 illustrative FFN experts. Routing defaults to top-4, with top-2 and four-token batch or single-token focus controls. Each token has independently normalized selected-expert weights and a weighted output. Its animated packets follow the same continuous paths used for dispatch, expert computation and return; paths remain specific to that token throughout the forward pass. Faint upper-triangle positions are masked; routing logits and token highlights are synthetic. Normalization layers, capacity limits and token dropping are omitted. This educational configuration follows the general sparse-routing formulation; it is neither Mixtral’s 8-expert/top-2 configuration nor MiniMax’s architecture.

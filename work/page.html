PaGE：空间感知凝视估计与轻量化蒸馏PaGE: turning visual representations into human-level gaze estimation

凝视估计需要同时利用场景语义、头部姿态与细微的视线线索。PaGE 设计了场景和头部两个视觉分支，使它们显式交换信息，再将大规模教师模型的表征蒸馏至轻量学生模型，并验证实际设备上的推理性能。我参与模型结构、蒸馏数据构建与训练验证。Understanding where a person looks requires scene semantics and fine head-and-gaze cues. PaGE explicitly connects these two visual streams, then distills a large teacher into practical students. My contribution spans architecture, data curation, and distillation experiments.

共享坐标系中的场景—头部特征交互Spatial alignment beyond feature concatenation

使用 DINOv3 分别编码场景与头部。Scene-head Interaction Module（SIM）先通过双向 Cross-Attention 交换信息，再由各分支的 Self-Attention 与 GEGLU MLP 处理。将头部 Patch 坐标映射回原始场景后，用共享坐标系中的 2D RoPE 表达相对位置与尺度。模型输出凝视热图，以及目标位于画面内的概率。DINOv3 encodes the scene and head separately. The Scene-head Interaction Module (SIM) exchanges information through bidirectional cross-attention, followed by branch-specific self-attention and GEGLU MLPs. Head patches are mapped back into scene coordinates, allowing unified-coordinate 2D RoPE to encode relative position and scale. Outputs are a gaze heatmap and an in-frame probability.

无凝视标注的蒸馏数据集Data design makes distillation scalable

使用 MPII 与 OpenImages V7 构建约 60.8 万图像、117 万头部区域的蒸馏数据集，通过 YOLOv5 检测头部，无需人工凝视标注。排除 COCO 来源数据以控制测试集重叠，用多样图像和教师表征监督补充有限的凝视标注数据。Curated approximately 608,000 images and 1.17 million head regions from MPII and OpenImages V7. YOLOv5 head detection provides crops without manual gaze labels; COCO sources are excluded to control evaluation overlap. Diverse images and teacher representations address the limited scale of gaze annotations.

大规模教师模型的表征蒸馏From a large teacher to practical students

以 PaGE ViT-H+ 为教师，在场景与头部两个分支上采用 Token 级 L1 与 Cosine 表征对齐，并加入骨干特征约束，随后进行下游监督微调。通过 Register Tokens、GEGLU、2D RoPE 与蒸馏目标的消融实验，区分结构设计、训练配方与数据的作用。A PaGE ViT-H+ teacher supervises token-level L1 and cosine feature alignment in both streams, with an auxiliary backbone-feature constraint, followed by supervised fine-tuning. Ablations of register tokens, GEGLU, 2D RoPE, and distillation objectives separate architectural, training, and data effects.

模型性能与设备推理验证From model capability to usable inference

蒸馏后的 ViT-B 在 GazeFollow、VAT 与 ChildPlay 的 9 项指标中，有 7 项超过论文采用的人类基线。ViT-S 在较低计算量下保留了较好的性能，并在 RTX 4090 / i9-14900K 上完成 BF16 延迟评测。公开模型、推理脚本与在线 Demo 可用于搭建和验证交互应用原型。The distilled ViT-B exceeds the reported human baselines on seven of nine metrics across GazeFollow, VAT, and ChildPlay. ViT-S retains strong performance at lower compute and is profiled in BF16 on an RTX 4090 and i9-14900K. Public checkpoints, inference scripts, and a demo make the model usable in interactive prototypes.

凝视估计与视觉注意的泛化问题The next question: gaze, or a broader notion of visual attention?

论文展示了动物、相机与动画角色等定性样例，并分析屏幕注视、标注误差和困难案例。我希望据此进一步研究语义理解、空间定位精度、数据覆盖和评价误差分别如何限制模型性能。The paper also explores qualitative examples involving animals, cameras, and animated characters, alongside screen gaze, annotation errors, and failure cases. These boundaries raise useful questions about semantic understanding, spatial precision, data coverage, and evaluation design.

下一步，追问什么？What comes next?

  • 什么样的交互结构能够结合全局语义与局部视觉线索？What interaction structure makes global semantics and local cues complement each other?
  • 多样但无凝视标注的数据，能否帮助小模型学习大模型的能力？Can diverse data without gaze labels transfer large-model capability to a small model?
  • 达到论文采用的人类基线后，数据偏差与评价误差是否会进一步限制性能？As models reach human baselines, do data bias and evaluation errors become the next bottlenecks?

PaGE：把视觉表征变成人类水平的凝视理解PaGE: turning visual representations into human-level gaze estimation

理解一个人看向哪里，需要同时读懂场景语义与细微的头部、视线线索。PaGE 让两个视觉分支显式交互，再把大教师的表征蒸馏进可运行在实际设备上的轻量学生模型。我的工作贯穿模型结构、数据构建与蒸馏验证。Understanding where a person looks requires scene semantics and fine head-and-gaze cues. PaGE explicitly connects these two visual streams, then distills a large teacher into practical students. My contribution spans architecture, data curation, and distillation experiments.

空间对齐，而不只是特征拼接Spatial alignment beyond feature concatenation

以 DINOv3 分别编码场景和头部。Scene-head Interaction Module（SIM）使用双向 Cross-Attention 交换信息，再通过各分支 Self-Attention 与 GEGLU MLP 处理；将头部 Patch 坐标映射回原始场景，用统一坐标系的 2D RoPE 表达相对位置和尺度。模型输出凝视热图及目标是否位于画面内的概率。DINOv3 encodes the scene and head separately. The Scene-head Interaction Module (SIM) exchanges information through bidirectional cross-attention, followed by branch-specific self-attention and GEGLU MLPs. Head patches are mapped back into scene coordinates, allowing unified-coordinate 2D RoPE to encode relative position and scale. Outputs are a gaze heatmap and an in-frame probability.

数据设计让蒸馏可扩展Data design makes distillation scalable

从 MPII 与 OpenImages V7 构建约 60.8 万图像、117 万头部区域的蒸馏数据，使用 YOLOv5 头部检测而无需人工凝视标注。排除 COCO 来源以控制测试重叠；将数据多样性与教师表征监督结合，缓解凝视标注规模受限的问题。Curated approximately 608,000 images and 1.17 million head regions from MPII and OpenImages V7. YOLOv5 head detection provides crops without manual gaze labels; COCO sources are excluded to control evaluation overlap. Diverse images and teacher representations address the limited scale of gaze annotations.

从大教师到轻量学生From a large teacher to practical students

以 PaGE ViT-H+ 为教师，在场景与头部两分支施加 Token 级 L1 与 Cosine 表征对齐，辅以骨干特征约束，再进行下游监督微调。结合 Register Tokens、GEGLU、2D RoPE 与蒸馏目标消融，区分结构设计、训练配方和数据带来的收益。A PaGE ViT-H+ teacher supervises token-level L1 and cosine feature alignment in both streams, with an auxiliary backbone-feature constraint, followed by supervised fine-tuning. Ablations of register tokens, GEGLU, 2D RoPE, and distillation objectives separate architectural, training, and data effects.

把能力落实到推理与交互From model capability to usable inference

蒸馏后的 ViT-B 在 GazeFollow、VAT 与 ChildPlay 的 9 项指标中有 7 项超过论文采用的人类基线；ViT-S 以较低计算量保留强性能，并在 RTX 4090 / i9-14900K 上完成 BF16 延迟评测。公开模型、推理脚本与在线 Demo，使算法可以直接进入交互应用原型。The distilled ViT-B exceeds the reported human baselines on seven of nine metrics across GazeFollow, VAT, and ChildPlay. ViT-S retains strong performance at lower compute and is profiled in BF16 on an RTX 4090 and i9-14900K. Public checkpoints, inference scripts, and a demo make the model usable in interactive prototypes.

继续问：学到的是凝视，还是更一般的视觉注意？The next question: gaze, or a broader notion of visual attention?

论文进一步展示动物、相机、动画角色等定性样例，并分析屏幕注视、标注误差和困难案例。我更关注这些边界提供的研究线索：语义理解、空间精度、数据覆盖和评价方式分别在限制什么。The paper also explores qualitative examples involving animals, cameras, and animated characters, alongside screen gaze, annotation errors, and failure cases. These boundaries raise useful questions about semantic understanding, spatial precision, data coverage, and evaluation design.

下一步，追问什么？What comes next?

  • 什么样的交互结构能让全局语义与局部线索真正互相补充？What interaction structure makes global semantics and local cues complement each other?
  • 无凝视标注的多样数据，能否把大模型能力迁移到小模型？Can diverse data without gaze labels transfer large-model capability to a small model?
  • 模型达到人类基线之后，数据偏差和评价误差会不会成为新的瓶颈？As models reach human baselines, do data bias and evaluation errors become the next bottlenecks?

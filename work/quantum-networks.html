跨越公里的量子纠缠，贯通物理与系统Kilometer-scale entanglement, built across physics and systems

从零搭建两套离子阱平台，贯通理论分析、精密光学、超高真空、电子测控与分布式实验。主导 12 km 离子—光子纠缠和 1.2 km 离子—离子纠缠，参与可编程光量子存储、DFS 长寿命存储与功能化双类型量子节点研究。I built two ion-trap platforms from scratch, connecting theoretical analysis, precision optics, ultra-high vacuum, electronics and distributed experiments. I led experimental work on 12 km ion–photon and 1.2 km ion–ion entanglement, and collaborated on programmable photonic memory, long-lived DFS storage and functional dual-type nodes.

要解决的问题The problem

远程纠缠是概率过程，光纤损耗和往返预报时间限制速率，存储退相干又不断消耗已建立的量子关联。我关注如何联合设计复用、编码、光学接口和控制时序，让链路生成速度与存储寿命满足可用网络的要求。Remote entanglement is probabilistic. Fiber loss and round-trip heralding time limit its rate while decoherence degrades stored correlations. I co-design multiplexing, encoding, optical interfaces and control timing to make link generation and memory lifetime compatible with useful networking.

我的工作与实现My contribution and implementation

  • 承担离子阱、激光/射频、超高真空及实验软件的设计与联调，建立装载、冷却、态制备、操控与层析流程，把系统问题转化为可测量的误差与稳定性指标。Designed and integrated ion traps, laser/RF systems, ultra-high vacuum and experiment software, establishing loading, cooling, state preparation, control and tomography workflows with measurable errors and stability.
  • 围绕原子单光子到单模光纤的耦合，结合高 NA 物镜、像差控制、衍射传播与高斯模式匹配开展光学设计，并将收集效率与偏振保持放回纠缠质量和链路速率中评估。Designed the atom-to-single-mode-fiber interface using high-NA objectives, aberration control, diffraction propagation and Gaussian mode matching, evaluating collection efficiency and polarization preservation through entanglement quality and link rate.
  • 主导混合多模式复用实验，在 12 km 光纤上实现最多 44 个时间模式和约 16 倍速率提升；进一步在两个远程离子节点实现十时间模式复用，获得 4.59 倍加速。Led hybrid-multiplexing experiments with up to 44 time bins and roughly 16-fold rate enhancement over 12 km, then extended temporal multiplexing to two remote ion nodes with ten time bins and a 4.59-fold speedup.
  • 合作参与可编程光量子存储、双类型通信/存储比特、DFS 编码与长时间纠缠保存，研究覆盖量子信息理论、量子算法基础、数值建模和实验验证。Collaborated on programmable photonic storage, dual-type communication/memory qubits, DFS encoding and long-lived entanglement, spanning quantum information theory, quantum-algorithm foundations, numerical modeling and experimental validation.

城域离子—光子链路Metropolitan ion–photon link

Nature Communications 17, 697：44 时间模式，12 km 光纤上 4.28 s⁻¹ 预报纠缠率，366 ms 存储相干时间。混合复用将速率提高约 16 倍。Nature Communications 17, 697: 44 time bins, a heralded rate of 4.28 s⁻¹ over 12 km and 366 ms memory coherence. Hybrid multiplexing improves the rate by about 16-fold.

高保真远程离子纠缠High-fidelity remote ion entanglement

PRL 137, 020803：1.2 km、十时间模式、4.59 倍加速与 95.9 ± 1.5% 保真度。该结果刷新当时长距离物质节点预报纠缠保真度纪录，获 Phys.org 专题报道。PRL 137, 020803: 1.2 km, ten time bins, 4.59-fold speedup and 95.9 ± 1.5% fidelity. The result set a fidelity record at the time for heralded matter-node entanglement over extended fiber distances and was featured by Phys.org.

可编程量子存储Programmable quantum memory

PRX 14, 021018：存储 72 个光量子比特，支持上千次连续随机读写，实现量子队列、栈与缓冲区。PRX 14, 021018: storage of 72 optical qubits and over a thousand sequential random-access write/read operations, enabling quantum queues, stacks and buffers.

DFS 与功能化量子节点DFS and functional network nodes

合作成果覆盖免串扰双类型节点、通信—存储比特纠缠门，以及离子阱 DFS 存储。PRL 工作在校正主要泄漏误差后获得超过两小时相干时间，PRA 工作在无泄漏事件后选择下实现约一小时纠缠逻辑态存储。Collaborative results cover crosstalk-free dual-type nodes, communication–memory entangling gates and DFS storage in trapped ions. The PRL work reports over two hours of coherence after correcting dominant leakage, while the PRA work obtains about one hour of entangled-logical-state storage by postselecting leakage-free events.

01 / RESEARCH STATEMENT

数据、表征、推理。 以及它们背后的架构。Data. Representations. Reasoning. And the architecture underneath.

我研究语言模型如何从数据中形成可调用的知识，以及这些表征如何支持推理。具体关注数据组织、知识读出、隐藏计算与架构归纳偏置；通过合成语料、冻结骨干探针和受控训练，区分记忆、访问与计算。我从零搭建 MiniTrainSys，快速把问题转成可复现的实验，并检验结论的适用范围。I study how language models acquire knowledge from data, make it accessible in their representations, and use it for computation. My questions connect data organization, knowledge readout, reasoning, and architectural inductive biases. I develop synthetic corpora, controlled training experiments, and frozen-backbone probes in MiniTrainSys, a framework I built from scratch, to turn these questions into reproducible tests with explicit limits.

实验物理训练让我习惯先拆解系统、隔离变量，再判断解释是否成立。从量子测控到分布式训练，我愿意深入底层，也重视把新想法迅速做成可测量的实验。Experimental physics shaped how I work: decompose a system, isolate variables, then test the explanation. From quantum control to distributed training, I work close to the fundamentals and build new experiments quickly.

01 / DATA

数据组织与知识获取Data organization and knowledge acquisition

事实集合不变时，改写、句序与采样方式如何改变知识的可读出性，而不只是训练文本的记忆率？When the facts stay the same, how do paraphrasing, sentence order, and sampling change what can be retrieved, beyond memorization of the training text?

在共享事实表上控制呈现方式，固定模型与优化配置；分别测量源文本记忆、属性首 Token 和完整属性读出，并记录训练 Token 预算的差异。Vary the presentation of a shared fact table while holding the model and optimization configuration fixed. Measure source-text memorization, first-token readout, and whole-value readout separately, and account for differences in training-token budgets.

实验依据与下一步Evidence & next questions +
已完成的对照Completed experiment

bioS 实验中，10 万人物、每人 6 项事实分别写成固定模板和改写/重排语料。两组均近乎记忆源文本，冻结骨干后的姓名输入首 Token 读出由 12.83% 变为 98.79%。In the bioS study, 100,000 people with six facts each appear in either fixed biographies or paraphrased, reordered text. Both models nearly memorize the source text, but name-only first-token readout with a frozen backbone changes from 12.83% to 98.79%.

两个同架构 MoE 各训练约 4B Token；计划 Token 预算相差 0.62%。结果对应冻结骨干 Q 探针，不是开放式问答生成准确率。Two matched MoEs are trained for approximately 4B tokens each; scheduled-token budgets differ by 0.62%. The result measures frozen-backbone Q-probe readout, not free-form question-answer generation.

继续检验Questions to test next

进一步分离改写、顺序和采样的作用，检验合成语料上的访问结构是否延伸到自然语料、不同训练配方与模型规模。Separate the effects of paraphrasing, order, and sampling, then test whether the access structure observed in synthetic data persists in natural corpora, different training recipes, and larger models.

02 / REPRESENTATIONS

知识表征与条件读出Knowledge representations and conditional readout

事实已经被记住时，为什么姓名状态能读出属性首 Token，却仍难以恢复完整属性？改变输入条件后，哪些信息重新变得可读？If a fact has been memorized, why can the name state reveal its first token while the complete value remains difficult to recover? What becomes readable when the input conditions change?

冻结骨干，比较传记前缀 P 探针与姓名输入 Q 探针；分开测量首 Token 和完整值，再结合真实首 Token 干预、重训探针与路由差分中的差分分析。Freeze the backbone and compare biography-prefix P probes with name-only Q probes. Separate first-token from whole-value readout, then use an oracle-first-token intervention, freshly trained probes, and route difference-in-differences analysis.

实验依据与下一步Evidence & next questions +
已完成的干预Completed intervention

在增强语料条件下，提供真实首 Token 并重新训练读出探针，完整属性读出由 45.28% 提升至 96.38%，显示后续值的可读出性依赖具体的 Token 与上下文条件。Under the augmented-data condition, supplying the true first token and training a fresh probe raises whole-value readout from 45.28% to 96.38%. This shows that access to the continuation depends on the token and context supplied to the probe.

Oracle 提供真实答案的首 Token；该实验不等于模型自主生成完整答案。探针留出的人物仍在骨干预训练中出现过。The oracle supplies the first token of the true answer; this is not autonomous generation of the complete answer. People held out from probe training were still present in backbone pretraining.

专家路由差异是访问过程的观测证据，不能据此断言某个专家独立存储某条事实。Routing differences characterize the access process; they do not establish that an individual expert stores a particular fact.

继续检验Questions to test next

进一步区分信息是否存在、何时可以访问，以及读出器能完成多少计算；通过输入与表示干预，检验这些解释是否依赖特定层、路由或架构。Distinguish whether information is present, when it is accessible, and how much computation the readout itself performs. Use input and representation interventions to test dependence on particular layers, routes, and architectures.

03 / REASONING

隐藏推理与错误恢复Hidden reasoning and error recovery

没有显式思维链时，模型如何完成多步计算？错误识别、回退与修正轨迹，如何改变推理的成功条件与失败方式？How does a model perform multistep computation without an explicit chain of thought? How do error recognition, backtracking, and correction trajectories change when reasoning succeeds or fails?

研究设计以合成任务控制依赖关系与推理深度，比较正确轨迹和带错误—修正步骤的训练轨迹；将最终答案、过程行为与隐藏状态分析分开评估。The experimental design uses synthetic tasks to control dependencies and reasoning depth, comparing correct trajectories with trajectories that include errors and corrections. Final answers, process behavior, and hidden-state analyses are evaluated separately.

实验依据与下一步Evidence & next questions +
已有实验基础Existing experimental foundation

MiniTrainSys 已具备从合成语料、Dense/MoE 预训练、可恢复检查点，到冻结骨干探针和评测的完整链路；已完成的机制实验目前聚焦知识读出。MiniTrainSys already connects synthetic corpora, Dense/MoE pretraining, resumable checkpoints, frozen-backbone probes, and evaluation. The completed mechanistic experiments currently concern factual readout.

进行中的研究方向Research in progress

沿 Physics of Language Models 的问题设置，推进 hidden reasoning 与 Regret/错误恢复研究；这些问题尚不作为已完成 bioS 实验的结论。I am developing studies of hidden reasoning and Regret-style error recovery, drawing on the problem formulations in Physics of Language Models. These remain ongoing directions, distinct from the completed bioS findings.

04 / ARCHITECTURE

架构归纳偏置与结构泛化Architectural inductive biases and structural generalization

相同训练信号经过不同的注意力、位置编码与路由结构，会形成怎样不同的组合能力与泛化边界？How do attention, positional encoding, and routing change the compositional capabilities and generalization limits learned from the same training signal?

在语言任务中，用上下文无关文法（CFG）控制层级、深度与组合，设计未见配置测试；在视觉任务中，通过交互模块、位置编码与蒸馏目标消融，分辨结构和训练配方的影响。For language tasks, use context-free grammars to control hierarchy, depth, and composition, and design tests on unseen configurations. For vision, ablate interaction modules, positional encodings, and distillation objectives to separate architectural effects from the training recipe.

实验依据与下一步Evidence & next questions +
已有架构研究Existing architecture work

在 PaGE 合作研究中参与场景—头部双向 Cross-Attention、统一坐标 2D RoPE 和蒸馏数据研发。论文通过结构与目标消融检验设计；完整蒸馏 ViT-B 在 3 个数据集的 9 项指标中有 7 项超过指定人类基线。In the collaborative PaGE project, I contributed to bidirectional scene–head cross-attention, unified-coordinate 2D RoPE, and distillation data. The paper tests these choices through architecture and objective ablations; the full distilled ViT-B exceeds the reported human baselines on seven of nine metrics across three datasets.

7/9 是完整模型的论文结果，不能归因于单个模块。PaGE 的场景与头部是两个视觉分支。Seven of nine is a paper-level result for the complete model, not an effect attributable to one module. PaGE's scene and head branches are both visual.

进行中的研究方向Research in progress

CFG 结构学习与泛化仍在推进；希望在明确训练预算和模型容量的条件下，检验模型是否学到可组合结构，以及哪些架构选择改变其泛化边界。Studies of CFG structure learning and generalization are ongoing. With training budgets and model capacity made explicit, I want to test whether models learn compositional structure and which architectural choices alter its generalization limits.

HOW I WORK

快速搭建，严格检验。Build quickly. Test carefully.

先把行为差异写成可证伪的假设，再决定最小对照。用合成数据固定事实与依赖关系，记录训练预算和配置；通过冻结骨干探针、输入干预与消融，区分记忆、读出和计算。自己搭建数据、训练与评测工具，快速迭代实验，同时保留原始测量、失败案例和结论边界。I start by turning a behavioral difference into a falsifiable hypothesis and choosing the smallest informative control. Synthetic data makes facts and dependencies explicit; training budgets and configurations are recorded. Frozen-backbone probes, input interventions, and ablations help separate memorization, access, and computation. I build the data, training, and evaluation tools myself to iterate quickly while retaining raw measurements, failure cases, and the conditions under which a conclusion holds.

HYPOTHESIS→CONTROL→INTERVENTION→MEASUREMENT→ITERATION

02 / INDUSTRIAL RESEARCH
MINIMAX / 预训练算法 · 实习PRETRAINING · INTERNSHIP

大规模预训练。 用实验理解 Scaling。Pretraining at scale. Scaling through experiments.

在 MiniMax 参与大规模 GPU 集群上的预训练算法研究，工作覆盖多级 Scaling Ladder、Scaling Law、Benchmark 与 BPB 评测、Document Packing、数据混合及数据管线协作。重点是把数据与训练策略转化为可比较的实验：明确模型规模和训练预算，追踪损失、任务表现与有效吞吐，并判断小规模观察能否在更大训练规模保持。At MiniMax, I work on pretraining algorithms in large GPU-cluster settings, contributing to model ladders, scaling analysis, benchmark evaluation, document packing, data mixtures, and the supporting data pipeline. My focus is to turn data and training choices into comparable experiments: specify the model scale and training budget, track loss and task-level behavior alongside effective throughput, and examine whether conclusions from smaller runs remain useful at larger scales. BPB is one evaluation method within this work.

05

对照实验与跨尺度结论稳定性Controlled experiments and cross-scale stability

研究中的核心问题是：某个数据或训练选择在小规模上有效，究竟是稳定趋势，还是预算、阶段和评测设置的产物？因此将模型规模、训练预算、数据配方和 Checkpoint 位置放在同一实验记录中，避免把不同条件下的最终分数直接比较。A central question is whether a choice that helps a smaller run reflects a stable trend or a particular budget, training stage, or evaluation setup. Model scale, training budget, data recipe, and checkpoint position therefore belong in the same experimental record, rather than being hidden behind final-score comparisons.

06 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

崔翟斌Zhaibin Cui

我关注大模型如何从数据中形成知识、表征与推理能力，以及这些能力如何随架构和训练规模变化。在 MiniMax 参与大规模预训练研究，同时独立搭建 MiniTrainSys，把模型行为转化为受控实验；在 PaGE 中探索空间感知表征与蒸馏。实验物理训练让我习惯追问机制、拆解变量，也愿意深入实现，把一个想法推进到可复现的结果和可用的系统。I study how language models acquire knowledge, representations, and reasoning from data, and how those capabilities change with architecture and training scale. At MiniMax, I contribute to large-scale pretraining research. Independently, I built MiniTrainSys to turn model behavior into controlled experiments, and contributed to spatially aware representations and distillation in PaGE. Experimental physics shaped how I work: ask what drives a result, isolate the variables, and build what is needed to turn an idea into reproducible evidence and a useful system.

INDUSTRIAL EXPERIENCE / MINIMAX

Pretraining research.
At production scale.

At MiniMax, I work on pretraining algorithms, data research and data engineering within the team’s large-scale training stack. I study how data composition and sequence construction shape learning dynamics and model capability, implement controlled comparisons, and contribute to shared data pipelines. Scaling-law analysis and capability evaluation help determine which findings justify a larger training run.

05 / BACKGROUND

Zhaibin Cui

I study how language models acquire knowledge from data, and how architecture and training scale affect representations and reasoning. I contribute to large-scale pretraining at MiniMax, built MiniTrainSys from scratch for controlled studies with synthetic data, and contribute to spatially aware visual modeling and distillation in PaGE. My training in experimental physics informs how I isolate variables, investigate unexpected results, and test explanations. I build the tools needed to run experiments quickly and record the conditions under which a result holds.
